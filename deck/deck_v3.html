Brief about the idea

One agent for everything that has to fit.

Tesselle is a voice-first shopping agent for things where fit matters - a desk organiser for a cramped desk, a blouse by Friday, 500 printed bags for an event.

You say it, show it, or look at it through smart glasses. Gemini writes the brief and the facts that decide fit, draws ideas into your own photo, finds real products in stores that deliver to your PIN, phones and emails real makers, and fills the store cart with cash on delivery - you place the order.

Every step is checked against your must-haves, and laptop, phone and glasses stay in sync on one project.

Mindflayer · Tesselle03
Opportunities

Not a chatbot that suggests links - an agent that does the job.

HOW IS IT DIFFERENT?
  • Shopping assistants recommend; Tesselle acts - calls makers, finds contacts, fills carts.
  • It covers ready-made and made-to-order in one journey.
  • Ideas are drawn into your own photo, not a stock render.
HOW DOES IT SOLVE IT?
  • Writes down the facts that decide fit and checks every option against them in code.
  • Reaches makers where they work - phone, WhatsApp, email - and turns answers into quotes.
  • Works across glasses, laptop and phone, in sync.
USP
  • Fit as a contract: must-haves never change without your yes.
  • A real-time AI phone agent (Gemini Live) that gets quotes from offline makers.
  • Steps planned per purchase - a desk and 500 bags get different journeys.
Today

Search → twenty tabs → guess the size → message makers one by one → return what doesn't fit.

Tesselle

One sentence → checked options from real stores and makers → you approve the order or the quote.

USER GUIDE
  1. Start talking on the homepage (or type) - e.g. "a desk organiser for my messy desk, under ₹5,000".
  2. Add a photo of the space when asked; confirm the brief.
  3. Say "show me ideas", then "shop the second one".
  4. Open Shop the look, add picks, and let the checkout agent fill the cart.
  5. Or try MetaGlass mode, or "Watch it run" for a guided demo.
