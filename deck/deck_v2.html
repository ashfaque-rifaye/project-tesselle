Project Tesselle09
09 · How your must-haves stay safe

Language models propose. Deterministic code decides.

  • Fit verdicts - fits / check a detail / doesn't fit, computed in code from structured requirements.
  • Quote deviations - every offered spec is diffed against the brief version it answers.
  • Versioned brief - nothing important changes without your decision; restore any version.
  • Honest uncertainty - photo sizes stay "measure to confirm"; unknown charges never join a total.

Project Tesselle12
12 · Trust by design

Honest by default. Safe by construction.

  • Every option links to a live product page or a real business; prices say "check the store page".
  • Must-haves change only through a labelled decision that creates a new brief version.
  • Ideas keep everything you own; only new products are added - and labelled as ideas.
  • Contacts come only from what businesses publish.
  • The agent says it's an AI on every call; recordings only with consent.
  • Messages, calls and orders need your yes; the final "Place order" tap is always yours.
  • Photos are scrubbed (location data, faces, documents) before a seller sees them.
  • Vertex AI only, secrets in Secret Manager, daily cost guard.
